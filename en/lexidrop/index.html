Learn vocabulary by playing: letters fall, you build the word

A word appears in English and you build its translation from falling letters before the stack reaches the red line. Rounds of a couple of minutes, no account, no sign-up.

Get it onGoogle Play

What it includes

  • 5 languages to learnSpanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese. Pick one the first time you open the app and change it from Settings whenever you like; progress in each language is saved separately.
  • From A1 to B2, topic by topic4 levels, 12 topics and 384 everyday words per language: home, food, family, city, travel, leisure, work, health, environment, society, science and opinion.
  • Mastered wordsA word is mastered once you get it right 3 times in a row. Master enough of them and you level up.
  • Power-upsFreeze stops the fall for 5 seconds, Bomb clears the tiles you do not need and Hint lights up the next letter.
  • Endless modeNon-stop words to beat your own record.
  • Dictionary and reviewKeeps every word you play, with synthesised-voice pronunciation. Words you miss, and mastered words you have not seen for a while, come back to your review list.
  • Daily streakEarn coins every day and special rewards on days 3, 7, 14 and 30.
  • Your wayLight or dark mode, text size up to 130%, vibration and a reduce-motion option. Interface in 6 languages.

How to play

  1. A word appears in your language.
  2. Build its translation from the falling letters before the stack hits the red line.
  3. Get it right 3 times in a row to master the word and level up.

Free, with ads

LexiDrop has no in-app purchases. It contains ads: rewarded ads are optional (for example, to continue a game, double your coins, get a power-up or unlock a topic for a few days) and there are discreet banner ads on the Dictionary and Settings screens. All your progress is stored only on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is LexiDrop free?

Yes. It has no in-app purchases; it is supported by ads, and rewarded ads are optional.

Do I need an account to play?

No. There is no sign-up and your progress is stored only on your device.

Which languages can I learn?

Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese. You can switch whenever you like and each one keeps its own progress.

Does it help me review?

Yes: the dictionary keeps the words you play and the ones you miss return to your review list.
