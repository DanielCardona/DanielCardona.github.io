What it includes

  • Receipt in 3 taps"Rent received", confirm, share via WhatsApp, email or any app.
  • Itemised amountsAmount in words and figures, with rent, charges advance or flat rate, and garage itemised separately.
  • Partial paymentThe app issues a receipt (not a quittance), then the quittance for the full term once the balance is paid.
  • Free, unlimited historyFind all your documents, send them again or duplicate them for next month.
  • Electronic delivery"Tenant's express agreement" checkbox, plus a sample agreement to sign.
  • Monthly reminderA notification to confirm the rent arrived, with no tenant names on the lock screen.
  • Furnished, flatshares, signatureFurnished or unfurnished lets, flatshares, finger-drawn signature.
  • Local backupExport and restore a JSON file. App in French and English; the documents themselves are always written in French.

How it works

  1. Add your property and tenant once.
  2. Each month, tap "Rent received" and confirm the payment.
  3. Share, print or save the PDF.

Free and transparent

Free: one property, unlimited receipts and the full history, supported by an ad banner on Home and History (never on your documents). Optional one-time Pro purchase, no subscription: several properties, whole-month batch generation, your logo on the PDF, yearly CSV export, no ads. The receipt itself is never paid or limited.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up or mandatory login. What you enter stays on your device.

Is the app free?

Yes: one property, unlimited receipts and the full history are free, with an ad banner. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, no subscription.

What happens with a partial payment?

The app issues a payment receipt, then the quittance for the full term once the balance is paid.

Is the app official?

No. It is an independent app, not affiliated with any public body. Generated documents use common wording and are not legal advice.

Where can I see how my data is handled?

In the privacy policy, linked below and available in the app.
